Library replacement and modification
FFmpeg is dynamically linked and may be replaced with compatible modified
versions for your own use. Preserve the ABI, exported symbols and library
filenames/load paths expected by PyAV. Locate the av and av.libs directories
inside the application bundle/folder; the manifest's native_files entries
identify the original wheel layout. Modified macOS bundles may need local
ad-hoc signing because binary modification invalidates the original signature.
No viewer license term prohibits reverse engineering for debugging changes
to these LGPL-covered libraries. No additional codec license fee is imposed
by this application.
